Bokas Tech · Last updated: 11 July 2026

How we use information

  • To reply to your support or sales questions

Sharing

We use Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) to send and receive messages. We may also use hosting and AI providers (for example Render and Google Gemini) to operate the chatbot. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep conversation records as long as needed to provide support and run our business, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

Your choices

You can stop messaging us at any time. To request access or deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.
